LoggerLink & iPad

  1. Connect to your station in LoggerLink.
  2. Collect the latest record from the relevant data table.
  3. Export the .dat file to Files, or use “Open In” Field Notes after installing the native app.
  4. Tap Autofill readings and check the station, logger time, field mapping, and units.
  5. Continue the sheet as you check and service the station.

Autofill never marks work as completed. Wet/dry tests, covered pyranometer readings, 1×/3× tip tests, and inspections are entered in the field.

Notes and arrival, departure and additional photos save on this device. Export a backup to Files to keep a separate copy; it includes all visits and their photo attachments. No automatic cloud backup is used.

On this iPad’s current software, LoggerLink requires full screen and cannot share Split View. Field Notes supports resizing and can run beside compatible apps. Use Files to transfer logger readings.

The native iPad app bundles the sheet for offline use. Camera capture is verified on your iPad. Station connectivity and LoggerLink handoff must be checked with your actual logger.
